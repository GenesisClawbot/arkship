Privacy notice · 7 September 2026

Your ship stays yours.

ARKSHIP is provided by Nikita Vorontsov. You can play without an account. Your save stays on your device, and optional playtest measurement starts off.

Your game and preferences

We store your current voyage, decisions, genome archive and sound/guide preferences locally. The browser uses its local storage. The mobile app uses the operating system's app storage. These are needed to resume play and keep your chosen settings; they are not automatically uploaded to us.

There is no cloud-save service or automatic cross-device synchronisation. Clearing browser storage or uninstalling the app removes local data, subject to any backups managed separately by your operating system.

Website hosting

The browser game and these pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not you enable ARKSHIP playtest statistics or sign in to GitHub. This hosting processing is separate from optional game analytics. GitHub's privacy statement explains its handling of this information.

Optional browser measurement

If you enable playtest stats in the browser game, Plausible Analytics receives visits and bounded game events: voyage starts, committed jumps and route categories, mutations, inherited strains grown, and endings. We send a fixed page address and event name. We do not send your typed voyage code, save, feedback text or screen recording.

Ordinary requests include an IP address and browser information. Plausible uses these to derive broad location and device/browser statistics and a daily identifier for visitor counts. It says it does not store the raw IP or full user agent, rotates the daily salt every 24 hours and stores aggregate analytics in the EU. See Plausible's data policy.

Optional mobile measurement

Mobile test builds without a playtest-stats switch do not collect game analytics. Where the iOS or Android app offers that switch, it uses Google Analytics for Firebase only when you enable it. It records game events and app/session usage, an app-instance identifier, device/app information and approximate location derived from the connection. This is installation-level measurement; it is not anonymous in the same way as a simple total counter.

We use it to understand whether people start voyages, where play stops and whether mutations and inherited strains are useful. Advertising identifier collection, advertising personalisation and cross-service advertising features are disabled. ARKSHIP does not send a name, email, typed voyage code, saved ship or genome bank to analytics. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

For optional mobile analytics, we have set user-level and event-level data retention to two months, with reset on new user activity switched off. Google applies retention changes after 24 hours and deletes expired data during a monthly process. These settings do not affect standard aggregated reports, which may remain available beyond two months. See Google's data-retention guidance.

Your choice

Optional measurement is off until you enable it. You can turn it off in the game's feedback panel at any time. The game continues to work if you decline or block analytics. Turning it off stops future collection by the game; it does not automatically remove previously recorded aggregate reports.

Reports and support

A shared voyage link contains its code and version, not your save. A voyage report contains the current ship, decisions, archive, version and bounded device/browser details. You choose whether and where to share that report using your browser or device's sharing tools. It is not sent as analytics.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support.

We use support messages and attachments for our legitimate interests in answering requests and fixing problems. We retain them while needed to resolve the request and related follow-up, then delete information no longer needed, unless a legal obligation or dispute requires retention. Optional analytics operates with your consent, which you can withdraw in the game.

Stores and service providers

Apple and Google handle mobile purchases, downloads and their own store services under their respective privacy terms. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Analytics providers process information for the functions described above; ARKSHIP does not sell your personal information.

We use Google's Gmail service for support email. Google may process messages and attachments on servers outside the UK and your country. Its privacy policy describes this processing, and its data-transfer information explains the applicable adequacy frameworks and contractual safeguards.

Questions and privacy requests

Contact chargen.official@gmail.com with a privacy question or a request about information you have sent to support. Depending on the applicable law and processing, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict its use, receive a portable copy or withdraw consent.

You have the right to object to our use of your personal information based on legitimate interests.

You may complain to your local data-protection authority. In the UK, you can make a complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

We will update this notice when the game's data handling changes. The date above identifies the current version.
