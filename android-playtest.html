What testing involves

Help test over at least 14 days.

We’re looking for around 20–25 Android players. Keep your Google Play test opt-in and group membership active for at least 14 consecutive days, even after the free download window closes. Try a few sessions and send honest feedback; no daily play quota or positive review is required.

  • Start your ark: play the first two jumps and tell us where you got stuck.
  • Try a voyage: which route did your ship make tempting, and what did you change to survive?
  • Launch a successor: if you bank a strain, try it next voyage and tell us what it makes possible.

Your browser and mobile saves remain separate, so starting the Android test will not move or replace a browser voyage.

Send feedback through ARKSHIP support. Read the privacy notice for how tester-group membership and messages are handled.
