Android closed test

Help the next ark survive.

Join the Android playtest and help shape ARKSHIP, a turn-based living spaceship survival game.

Paid Android closed test · Free-code requests open

1. Join the tester group Play in your browser
01

Join the tester group

Join with the same Google account you use for Google Play on your Android device. Group membership makes that account eligible for the closed test.

Joining the group signs you up for eligibility. It does not install ARKSHIP or opt you into the Google Play test.

02

Opt in on Google Play

After joining the group, open the testing page with that same Google account and choose “Become a tester”. Before paying for ARKSHIP, request a free code in step 03 below.

ARKSHIP is a paid app. Test regions and prices: UK £3.99, US US$3.99, Canada C$4.99, Australia A$5.99 and New Zealand NZ$6.99. Check the price shown by Google Play before purchasing. The developer is listed as OutfitPilot.

2. Opt in on Google Play

If you see “App not available”, check that you have joined the tester group, then switch to that same Google account on the testing page and in Google Play.

03

Request a free code before paying

We have 12 single-use Google Play codes for the first 12 complete, eligible requests, processed in receipt order. One code per person, while codes remain.

Email your Google Play account address and country, and confirm that you joined the tester group and opted into the closed test with that same account. If a code is available and you are eligible, we will reply privately with one unique code. An automated assistant checks code requests daily; replies are not instant.

3. Request a free code by email

Opening the link prepares an email draft. You choose whether to send it; ARKSHIP does not send it automatically.

04

Redeem, then install

If you receive a code, redeem it with the same Google account before purchasing, then return to Google Play to install ARKSHIP.

4. Redeem on Google Play

Codes expire on 30 November 2026 at 23:59 GMT. Each code can be used once, has no cash value and must not be sold. Redemption depends on Google Play device, region and age eligibility.

If you do not receive a code, you can still purchase ARKSHIP at the price Google Play shows for your region.

What testing involves

Help test over at least 14 days.

Once you opt into the Google Play closed test, keep your test opt-in and group membership active for at least 14 consecutive days. Try a few sessions, including a voyage and a successor, and tell us about bugs or confusing decisions. There is no daily play quota, and we do not require a positive review.

Your browser and mobile saves remain separate, so starting the Android test will not move or replace a browser voyage.

Send feedback through ARKSHIP support. Read the privacy notice for how tester-group membership and messages are handled.
